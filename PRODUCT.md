# Product

*Inferred from the written brief (no interview was possible); assumptions are labelled.*

**What it is.** Second Brain is a private, offline-first Android app for catching loose thoughts, links and photos in seconds and finding them again later. Everything is categorised on-device by deterministic rules. No LLMs, no accounts, no cloud.

**Register / mode.** Operate: a tool used in passing moments (standing in a shop, reading on a train, between meetings). Scanability, speed and native expectations outrank expression.

**Primary user.** One person, capturing one-handed, often interrupted. (Assumption.)

**Success.** Capture takes under five seconds and never requires a decision. Retrieval works through Recent, Library or Search. Any automatic assignment can be reviewed and changed in two taps, and a manual choice is permanent.

**Tone.** Calm, private, unhurried. A well-kept notebook, not a dashboard. Copy is short, plain, first-person-friendly ("Saved", "Couldn't tell where this belongs").

**Anti-references.** Productivity-app gamification, tag soup, folders you must choose from, bright multi-colour category chips, and AI sparkle iconography (the app is deliberately not AI).

**Principles**
1. Saving never waits on analysis, and nothing is lost if analysis fails.
2. The app decides so the user doesn't have to, and shows its reasoning when asked.
3. A manual choice always beats an automatic one.
4. One primary action per screen.
5. Quiet by default; colour carries meaning, not decoration.
